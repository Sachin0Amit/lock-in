About

The Social Science Department

SSC School of Social Sciences
Top 10 research output among social science schools

The School of Social Sciences runs Aurelia's largest fieldwork operation: every undergraduate completes at least one supervised village or ward study, and the school's survey unit collects panel data across three states for its research centres.

Economists, sociologists, political scientists and policy scholars teach side by side, so a single seminar can move from a household survey to a fiscal model to a drafting exercise for a ministry memo — the mix recruiters come back for.

  • Supervised field study in every undergraduate programme
  • Nine funded research centres on campus
  • Longitudinal survey unit across three states
  • Joint seminars shared by four disciplines

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.

CATI phone-bank, tablet-based field tools and a 40-seat data-cleaning studio.

Rapid prototyping space for public-service ideas with whiteboard walls and law library access.

Mapping workstations with open census and satellite data for coursework and research.

Decision-experiment rooms with eye-tracking and response-time software.
