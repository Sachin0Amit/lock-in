About

The Education Department

EDU School of Education
34 partner practice schools across the city

The School of Education trains teachers the way teaching deserves: every student records lessons in the micro-teaching studios from week one, reviews them with mentors, and spends two full terms inside the school's 34 partner practice schools.

Research strengths in curriculum studies, educational psychology and inclusive education feed directly into teacher preparation — and the school's action-research requirement sends every graduate out with evidence that their classroom interventions worked.

  • Micro-teaching studios used from week one
  • Two terms inside partner practice schools
  • Action-research project for every graduate
  • Inclusive-education specialisation track
