About

The Mathematics Department

MAT School of Mathematics & Statistics
31 medals at national olympiads since 2015

The School of Mathematics & Statistics believes mathematics is a contact sport: weekly problem nights, a student-run seminar and a two-semester reading course mean students prove things, not just watch proofs. The school's actuarial and data electives are co-designed with industry actuaries and statisticians.

Graduates progress to research fellowships, actuarial traineeships, quantitative finance and teaching — with one of the university's highest placement rates and an olympiad pipeline that has produced 31 medal-winning problem-solvers since 2015.

  • Weekly open problem nights run by students
  • Reading courses in place of some lecture courses
  • Actuarial electives co-designed with industry
  • Olympiad and contest coaching pipeline
