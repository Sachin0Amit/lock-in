About

The Chemistry Department

CHE School of Chemistry
Green Labs certified teaching laboratories

The School of Chemistry believes chemists are made at the bench: every theory course runs beside a wet-lab, and undergraduates graduate with hundreds of hours of supervised synthesis, analysis and instrumentation time, logged against the school's safety framework.

Research spans organic synthesis, polymers, catalysis and analytical science, with 38 industry MoUs translating student projects into product development. The green-chemistry centre audits every teaching lab's waste stream — a policy the school wrote itself.

  • Wet-lab attached to every theory course
  • Green-chemistry audits across teaching labs
  • NMR and chromatography access for undergraduates
  • 38 industry MoUs for project translation

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.
