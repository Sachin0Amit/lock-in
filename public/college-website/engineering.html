About

The Engineering Department

ENG School of Engineering & Technology
Ranked #7 among India's young engineering schools

The School of Engineering & Technology is Aurelia's largest school, home to more than 4,800 students across mechanical, civil, electronics and computing disciplines. Teaching is built around a build-first philosophy: every core course pairs lecture hours with bench hours, and every laboratory is sponsored, staffed and reviewed by an industry partner.

Students complete a two-semester capstone for a real client, present it at the annual Impetus showcase, and graduate with a transcript that reads like a portfolio — prototypes, patents and publications included. The school's placement office runs a year-round studio that has kept campus placement above 90 per cent for nine consecutive years.

  • AICTE-approved and NBA-accredited B.Tech programmes
  • 40+ active industry MoUs and sponsored laboratories
  • Two-semester industry capstone with real clients
  • 1:12 faculty-mentor to student ratio in project years
