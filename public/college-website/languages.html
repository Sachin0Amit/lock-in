About

The Languages Department

LNG School of Languages & Linguistics
12 languages taught under one school roof

The School of Languages & Linguistics teaches twelve languages — nine foreign and three classical Indian — and treats each as a living discipline rather than a service course. Native-speaker fellows anchor conversation classes, and the tandem lounge pairs learners across languages every evening.

Graduates leave with a certified proficiency level in every language they study, a translation or teaching portfolio, and — for interpreters — booth hours logged in the school's interpretation suite that doubles as a regional conference venue.

  • Twelve languages from Arabic to Sanskrit on one campus
  • Native-speaker conversation fellows for every language
  • Certified CEFR-aligned proficiency for all graduates
  • Interpretation booths used by real conferences

Acoustic analysis stations with ultrasound and electropalatography for speech research.

Annotated corpora in twelve languages with concordance and alignment software.
