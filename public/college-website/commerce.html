About

The Commerce Department

COM School of Commerce
1,400 cases in the India-written case archive

The School of Commerce treats accounting as a language and markets as literature: students run the school's market-data lab, complete certified modules in taxation and audit, and file real practice returns at the student tax clinic every season.

Its analytics and fintech electives are co-taught with industry accountants and bankers, and the school's case archive — 1,400 teaching cases written for Indian companies — keeps classrooms anchored in the domestic economy.

  • Professional-certification embedded electives
  • Student tax clinic serving real filers
  • 1,400 Indian business teaching cases
  • Market-data lab with live terminals
