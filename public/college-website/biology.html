About

The Biology Department

BIO School of Biological Sciences
6 field research stations in five habitats

The School of Biological Sciences teaches biology as a continuum: the same cohort that sequences a gene in the morning genomics lab maps its organism's habitat at the hill field station the same term. Six field stations — hill, wetland, coast, grassland, urban and forest — anchor the school's field culture.

Research and coursework span biotechnology, ecology, genetics and animal behaviour, with a conservatory, tissue-culture facility and behavioural-observation lab on campus, and a genomics suite whose sequencer is student-operable after certification.

  • Six field stations across five habitat types
  • Student-operable sequencer after certification
  • Campus conservatory as a living library
  • Behaviour and ecology tracks alongside biotech

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.

Genomics & Sequencing Lab

Benchtop sequencer and bioinformatics workstations, student-operated after certification.

Greenhouse & Conservatory

Living collection of 900 taxa used for physiology, breeding and taxonomy coursework.

Tissue Culture Facility

Laminar-flow suites for plant and cell culture with contamination-audit training.

Wildlife Field Station

Hill-campus station with dorms, trail network and long-term biodiversity plots.

Behavioural Observation Lab

Arena and acoustic booths for ethology experiments and field-video analysis.

Bioinformatics Suite

Computing room with pipelines for assembly, variant calling and biodiversity analytics.
