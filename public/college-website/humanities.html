About

The Humanities Department

HUM School of Humanities
Ranked #12 among India's humanities schools

The School of Humanities is Aurelia's founding school, opened with the university in 1962. Its classrooms still sit around the original neem courtyard, and its seminar tables remain deliberately small: no lecture in the school exceeds 40 students, and every final-year student writes a supervised dissertation.

Faculty research runs from classical Indian philosophy to migration studies and digital humanities, and students are co-authors, not assistants — the school's undergraduate journal Marginalia has published 42 student essays across eleven volumes.

  • Seminar classrooms capped at 40 students
  • Rare manuscripts archive with student access
  • Supervised dissertation for every finalist
  • Undergraduate research journal, eleven volumes strong

Infrastructure

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.

Rare Manuscripts Archive

Climate-controlled vault of 3,100 manuscripts and early printed editions, open to supervised students.

Digital Humanities Studio

Text-corpus workstations, OCR and mapping software for project-based humanities research.

Open-Air Amphitheatre

Stone-tiered courtyard theatre for readings, classical drama and the annual philosophy play.

Writing & Rhetoric Centre

Drop-in coaching on argument, style and structure for essays and dissertations across the university.

Film Studies Archive

Restored world cinema prints with a 40-seat screening room and annotation consoles.

Debate & Dialogue Chamber

Walled debate room used for the inter-school fortnightly parliamentary debates.
