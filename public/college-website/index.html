About

Why Aurelia

A private university built on the conviction that disciplines are learned by practising them — with the facilities, faculties and cohorts that make practice possible.

Seminar-scale teaching

No first-year lecture at Aurelia exceeds 60 students; tutorials run in groups of twelve.

Research from year one

Undergraduates join funded projects early — as co-authors, assistants and paid interns.

Practice-first pedagogy

Clinics, moots, consulting briefs, field terms and internships are built into every programme.

One campus, fifteen schools

Engineers share makerspaces with designers; lawyers moot with management case-rooms.

A green, walkable campus

60 acres of trees, a botanical conservatory and six field stations beyond the gates.

Outcomes that travel

92 per cent of graduates placed or in further study within six months, nine years running.
