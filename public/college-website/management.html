About

The Management Department

MGT School of Management
40 ventures incubated by the entrepreneurship cell

The School of Management runs a live-project curriculum: 82 client briefs are in progress this academic year, every student negotiates at least one, and the MBA capstone is defended before a panel of client managers rather than faculty alone.

Teaching is case-first — 60 per cent of contact hours — supported by a decision-science lab, a leadership assessment centre and an entrepreneurship cell that has incubated 40 student ventures. Exchange agreements send a third of each MBA cohort abroad for a term.

  • 82 live client projects this academic year
  • Case-method majority in contact hours
  • Consulting capstone defended to clients
  • 40 student ventures incubated on campus
