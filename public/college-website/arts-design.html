About

The Arts & Design Department

ART School of Arts & Design
11 exhibitions mounted by students each year

The School of Arts & Design runs a studio culture: first-year students keep a sketchbook journal from fortnight one, crits are public and weekly, and the university gallery mounts eleven student exhibitions a year alongside the flagship degree show.

Craft facilities — printmaking, ceramics, darkroom, textiles — sit beside digital suites for motion capture and interaction design, so painters learn code ethics and interaction designers learn paper and clay. Every programme ends with a public exhibition, hung and marketed by the students themselves.

  • Weekly public crits and studio culture
  • Eleven student exhibitions every year
  • Craft and digital suites side by side
  • Student-hung, student-marketed degree show

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.

Printmaking & Etching Studio

Etching, lithography and screen presses with acid rooms and paper conservation.

Photography Darkroom

Black-and-white darkroom with film scanning suite for the photography electives.

Motion Capture Stage

Capture volume used by animation, interaction and performing-arts students.

Ceramics & Kiln Yard

Wheel rooms, kilns and glaze lab for ceramics electives and open studio nights.

University Gallery

Two-hundred-metre exhibition hall hosting eleven student shows and the degree exhibition.

Textile & Surface Lab

Weaving looms and digital textile printers shared with the design programmes.
