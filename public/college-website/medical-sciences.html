About

The Medical Sciences Department

MED School of Medical Sciences
900 beds in the attached teaching hospital

The School of Medical Sciences teaches medicine the way it is practised: anatomy begins in the dissection hall, physiology is measured on real tracings, and clinical reasoning starts in year one through case-based seminars. Students rotate through the attached 900-bed teaching hospital, urban community clinics and rural health centres before they ever sit for their licensing steps.

Graduates match into residency programmes across internal medicine, surgery, paediatrics and family medicine — supported by a structured mentoring scheme, a clinical-skills portfolio reviewed every semester, and a research requirement that has produced student co-authored publications every year since 2018.

  • Bedside rotations from the third year
  • Simulation centre before live procedures
  • Community and rural health postings
  • Student co-authored research every year

Infrastructure

Facilities & Resources

Labs, wards, simulation suites and centres that the school's students use day to day.

Teaching Hospital

900-bed attached hospital with nine specialty wards and round-the-clock emergency services.

Anatomy & Dissection Hall

Cadaveric dissection tables, prosection library and an imaging-ready anatomy model studio.

Clinical Simulation Centre

High-fidelity manikins, a mock operating theatre and debrief rooms for procedure training.

Central Instrumentation Lab

Shared biochemistry, molecular biology and pathology analyser suite for student projects.

Medical Skills & Suture Lab

Bench stations for suturing, catheterisation, CPR and OSCE practice with recording feedback.

Community Health Centre

Adopted urban and rural centres hosting the school's posting rotations and outreach camps.
