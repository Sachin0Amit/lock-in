About

The Physics Department

PHY School of Physics
45 cm campus observatory telescope aperture

The School of Physics teaches physics the way it is practised: undergraduates handle lasers, vacuum systems and cryostats in their first year, join a research group by their third, and many appear as co-authors before they graduate.

The school's observatory, photonics labs and materials-growth facility serve both teaching and research, and its 46 ongoing projects are funded by national science agencies and industry alike. Night-sky open houses are a campus institution.

  • First-year students run real experiments, not demos
  • Undergraduate co-authorship on research papers
  • Campus observatory with public viewing nights
  • 46 funded projects across optics, materials and astrophysics

Infrastructure

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.

Astronomical Observatory

45 cm reflector with CCD imaging and remote-observing console for coursework.

Optics & Photonics Lab

Laser tables, spectrometers and fibre optics benches for lab courses and projects.

Materials Growth Facility

Thin-film deposition and crystal-growth suites with characterisation instruments.

Computational Physics Cluster

Simulation nodes for astrophysics, condensed matter and fluid-dynamics modelling.

Instrumentation Workshop

Machine and electronics shop where students build experiment rigs from scratch.

Low-Temperature Physics Lab

Cryostat stations down to 4 K for transport and magneto-optical measurements.
