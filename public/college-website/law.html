About

The Law Department

LAW School of Law
18 trophies at national moot courts since 2010

The School of Law believes lawyers are made by arguing: the moot-court complex runs a competitive docket every week, every student drafts live documents for the legal-aid clinic, and sitting judges routinely preside over the final rounds of the school's moot championships.

Its 18 national moot trophies sit beside a 100 per cent pro-bono requirement: every graduate completes supervised legal-aid service. Cyber law, constitutional litigation and arbitration electives reflect the school's practitioner-teacher mix — two-thirds of faculty argue in court.

  • Weekly competitive moot docket in-house
  • Two-thirds of faculty are practising counsel
  • Mandatory supervised legal-aid service
  • 18 national moot trophies since 2010

Facilities & Resources

Labs, studios, libraries and centres that the department's students use day to day.

Moot Court Complex

Two courtrooms with benches, witness stands and recording used weekly by the competitive docket.

Legal Aid Clinic

Walk-in clinic where students, supervised by counsel, serve the campus and neighbourhood.

Dispute Resolution Chamber

Arbitration and mediation suites with trained co-mediators for ADR coursework.

Case Law Digital Library

Full-text judgments, statutes and commentaries with citation-management training.

Client Interviewing Studio

Recording suites where students practise client conferences with standardised clients.

Centre for Legal Policy

Research centre producing briefs and legislative commentary with student research assistants.
