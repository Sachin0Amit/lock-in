**The honest fold:** the backlog's core line "Evolutionary" and the
zoology line "Evolution" share one register — one evolution book owns the
whole evolutionary story for the shelf (the animal-side examples live in
its chapters; the plant-side cross-references plant-genetics). Everything
else maps one-to-one from the backlog's 24 lines (9 + 7 + 8 = 24 lines →
23 books with the fold).

## Sibling views and the no-duplication contract

The biology shelf owns the **general registers**: microbiology owns the
organism-level microbial story (the clinical-disease register is the
medical shelf's medical-microbiology — cross-referenced as sibling views,
exactly as the chemistry shelf's biochemistry already cross-references
"the future Medical-and-Biology shelves' biochemistry registers" in its
own positioning); immunology owns the general immune machinery (the
clinical disease register belongs to clinical-immunology); animal-anatomy
and animal-physiology own the comparative registers (the human clinic
belongs to the medical shelf's anatomy and medical-physiology); the
metabolism chemistry beneath is the chemistry shelf's biochemistry
(owned there, cross-referenced here). Lesson-id prefixes verified unused
across all 144 written lesson ids and distinct from every reserved
prefix (near-misses priced: `micro-` is microeconomics', `behav-` is
behavioural-economics', `arch-` is archaeology's; the shelf takes
`bact-`, `etho-`, `pgen-` … per the reservation table in the generator).
