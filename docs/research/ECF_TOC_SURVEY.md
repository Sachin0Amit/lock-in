### The no-duplication contract (cross-references, not folds)

- **statistics-for-economics** vs the mathematics shelf's `statistics`
  and `probability-theory`: the economics-application register (index
  numbers, time series, official data) vs the pure-theory side.
- **business-mathematics** vs `quantitative-aptitude`,
  `differential-calculus`, `integral-calculus`, `linear-algebra`: the
  commercial register (EMI, annuities, marginal calculus for commerce)
  cross-references the pure side at each borrowing.
- **econometrics** vs mathematics `statistics`: regression-and-causal
  register vs distribution theory.
- **marketing / hrm / operations** vs engineering `industrial-
  engineering` and the mathematics `optimization-and-operations-
  research`: the functional-management register vs the pure OR side.
- **banking / monetary-economics**: the institutional-operational
  register vs the policy-theory register — the division is stated in
  both books' positioning.
- **behavioural-economics** vs the humanities shelf's `psychology`:
  the economics-side critique cross-references the discipline's home.
