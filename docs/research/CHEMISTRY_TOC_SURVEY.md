### The honest folds (5)

1. **General Chemistry → foundation-chemistry.** The survey register is
   the school ladder's own name (Atkins & Jones' Chemical Principles arc
   — contents read this pass — is exactly the foundation/general
   content in one volume).
2. **Electrochemistry → physical-chemistry** (a full chapter set: cell
   architecture, EMF, Nernst, Kohlrausch, Faraday, corrosion — the
   standard Atkins Focus arc).
3. **Chemical Kinetics → physical-chemistry** (a full chapter set: rate
   laws, integrated equations, Arrhenius, catalysis — the standard
   Atkins Focus arc).
4. **Advanced Chemical Thermodynamics → physical-chemistry** (the
   graduate register as the thermodynamics-advanced chapter: partial
   molar quantities, chemical potential, activity, phase rule).
5. **Materials → the engineering shelf's materials-science book** (the
   Callister register, planned 2026-11-16 — cross-referenced OUT of the
   chemistry shelf, no new book: the QA-creation precedent inverted, an
   honest fold-out).

### The no-duplication contract (cross-references, not folds)

- **physical-chemistry** vs physics `heat-and-thermodynamics` (the pure
  side), mathematics `optimization-and-operations-research` (the pure
  kinetics mathematics) — the chemistry dress is the owner here.
- **quantum-chemistry** vs physics `quantum-mechanics`: the chemist's
  register (orbitals, MO theory, term symbols) vs the formalism — each
  book's positioning names the boundary.
- **environmental-chemistry** vs humanities `environmental-studies`
  (the school AEERS register) and engineering `environmental-
  engineering` (the applied plant side) — the chemistry-side register
  owns the pollutant-and-cycle chemistry.
- **biochemistry** vs the organic book's biomolecules school register
  vs the future Medical/Biology shelves' sibling registers — the
  chemistry-side view (structure-and-mechanism first) with the
  cross-references stated.
- **analytical-chemistry** vs physics `experimental-physics` (the
  instrument-side) and mathematics `statistics` (the pure inference
  side).
- **industrial-chemistry** vs the engineering shelf (chemical
  engineering is on the engineering pass's investigate-later list —
  the boundary is recorded honestly in both places).

## The registers read this pass (with URLs)

1. **GATE CY (Chemistry) 2026 syllabus PDF** (IIT Guwahati):
   https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/doc/GATE2026_Syllabus/CY_2026_Syllabus.pdf
   — Section 1 Physical Chemistry (postulates of quantum mechanics,
   operators, time-dependent and time-independent Schrodinger
   equations; thermodynamics; electrochemistry; kinetics), the
   Organic and Inorganic sections (incl. bioinorganic: ion transport,
   oxygen binding, metalloenzymes) — pins the university spine (the
   GATE 2027 mirror at gate2027.iitm.ac.in corroborates the section
   structure).
2. **CSIR UGC NET Chemical Sciences syllabus PDF** (the official
   csirhrdg.res.in file):
   https://csirhrdg.res.in/SiteContent/ManagedContent/ContentFiles/20201221135711180chemical_science_syllabus.pdf
   — Inorganic / Physical / Organic + the interdisciplinary topics
   (chemistry in nanoscience and technology, catalysis and green
   chemistry, medicinal chemistry, supramolecular chemistry) — pins
   the research register.
3. **NEET (UG) 2026 syllabus PDF** (NTA notice — the same PDF read for
   the Physics pass): https://nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260108180635.pdf
   — the chemistry register (Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry,
   Structure of Atom, Classification of Elements and Periodicity,
   Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium,
   Redox Reactions, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics,
   the s/p/d-and-f blocks, Coordination Compounds, Haloalkanes and
   the organic families, Biomolecules, Environmental Chemistry) —
   pins the school-competitive spine. The JEE Main 2026 syllabus
